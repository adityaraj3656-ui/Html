Facts:1 🐙The amazing octopus

The octopus is a fascinating creature known for its intelligence and unique abilities. It has three hearts, blue blood, and can change color and texture to blend into its surroundings. Octopuses are also capable of solving complex puzzles and escaping from enclosures, showcasing their remarkable problem-solving skills.

How smart are Octopus?

Octopus can open jars, solve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent invertebrates on Earth.

Fact:2 ⚡Lightning Strikes

Lightning strikes the Earth about 100 times every single second. This is roughly 8 millions lightning strikes per day!

How hot is lightning?

A single bolt of lightning is 30,000 Kelvin - Five times hotter than the surface of Sun.

Can lightning strike twice?

Yes! Lightning absoulety can strike and damage at the same place more than once, espically the tall structures like towers and trees

💧Fact:3 The deep Oceans

More than 80% of the world's oceans remain unexplored. Scientists believe there are millions of spieces living in the deep of sea that we have never discovered

How deep does the ocean go?

The deepest point on the Mariana Trench, which reaches a depth of about 11 kilometres - deep enough to fit Mount Everest inside with the room to spare!

Fun comparison

If you placed Mount Everest at the bottom of the Mariana Trench, its peak would still be more than 2 kilometres underwater.
